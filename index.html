The Union Mikvah

The
Union
Mikvah

The Union Mikvah was built to serve the heimish community in Union and the surrounding areas with a beautiful, modern mikvah built to the highest standards of halachic hiddur.

It is the only mikvah in Central New Jersey built to this level of excellence, serving families from Union and many surrounding towns and cities.

With a warm, welcoming atmosphere and meticulous attention to every halachic detail, the Union Mikvah provides a place where every visitor can immerse with comfort, dignity, and peace of mind.

Sponsorship
opportunities

There are many opportunities to partner in this important mitzvah. Sponsorships help support the ongoing operation, maintenance, and upkeep of the mikvah, while allowing you to have a meaningful share in this vital institution. Whether through sponsoring a specific area, an operating expense, or making a general dedication, every sponsorship helps ensure that the Union Mikvah continues serving the community at the highest standards.

Testimonials

Mordechai M. made a donation to the Mikvah as a zechus to find the right home to purchase. The very next day, a broker showed him a house that turned out to be exactly what he was looking for — and, to top it off, he was able to purchase it at a great price.

After eight years of waiting for a child, Naomi's classmates came together to dedicate a room in the Mikvah in her zechus. Nine months later, Naomi was holding her own baby boy in her arms.

Baruch made a generous donation toward the completion of the Mikvah, with a heartfelt wish that it should be a zechus for him to be blessed with more children after a long break. A year later, his family was blessed with triplets. A remarkable yeshuah and a powerful reminder of the incredible zechus of supporting a mikvah.
